# Line data notices

## SUBTLEX-US word list

`subtlex-us-words.txt` is generated from `index.json` in:

https://github.com/words/subtlex-word-frequencies

The original source contains 74,286 word forms derived from SUBTLEXus,
a corpus of American English movie subtitles containing approximately
51 million words.

The generated project word list contains 61,715 unique lowercase
alphabetic word forms selected from the source dataset.

Original dataset information:

https://www.ugent.be/pp/experimentele-psychologie/en/research/documents/subtlexus
